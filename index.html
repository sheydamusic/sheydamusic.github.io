About

Sheyda is a classically trained singer, composer and songwriter whose voice lies between dramatic soprano and mezzo-soprano. Her journey began with Pink Floyd, whose philosophical lyrics opened a new world to her and led her to pick up the guitar and write her first songs. As the only musician in a non-musician family, she worked to rent piano rooms for practice and eventually bought her own piano.

Today she writes music that often arrives fully formed in vivid dreams or on long, solitary walks, and moves between cinematic neoclassical instrumentals and dreamy indie songs in English. She composes and produces her own work, and performs on piano and guitar.
